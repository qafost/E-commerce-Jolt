Shipping & returns

Orders usually leave the studio within 2 business days. Egypt deliveries take 1–3 days. International parcels take 7–14 days depending on customs.

Standard shipping is calculated at checkout. Free shipping on orders over $150.

If something arrives damaged or wrong, email us within 14 days with your order number. We will replace it or refund the item. Return postage is covered for our mistakes; otherwise a small return label fee applies.

Sale items can be returned unused, with tags, in original packaging.
