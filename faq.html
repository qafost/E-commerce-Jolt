Questions

Do I need a real card to checkout?

This demo store keeps orders on your device. Use any 12+ digit number in the card field. No payment is charged.

Where is my order saved?

In this browser, under your account email. Clearing site data removes the basket and order history.

Can I change an order?

Write through the contact page with the order number from your account page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. See the shipping page for timing.
