A quieter kind of store

Stuffsus started as a short list of objects we actually use: sound that travels, light that stays warm, bags and textiles that survive a week of real life.

We keep the catalog small on purpose. Every piece is chosen for materials, repairability, and a price that does not need a fake discount to make sense.

Orders pack from Cairo and ship worldwide. Questions go to the contact page — a person reads them.
